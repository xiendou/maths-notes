\chapter*{About these notes}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{About these notes}
These editable study notes are based on \emph{Analysis} by Christopher
Brocklebank and James Yang (October 2024), following Prof.~Had\v zi\'c's
UCL Analysis 1 course in 2024--25. They are a rewritten companion, rather
than a verbatim transcription or official course material.

The four chapters follow the source's order. Definitions, principal results,
worked examples and proof arguments have been rewritten in consistent notation.
Repeated attempts at proofs have been combined.

Obvious mathematical errors have been corrected. Some arguments left unfinished
or assigned as exercises in the source are supplied here; these additions are
identified where relevant. The source's missing figure placeholders are not
reproduced. The accompanying README records the main editorial decisions.

Throughout, $\mathbb N=\{1,2,3,\ldots\}$ and all sequences are real unless stated
otherwise. A statement labelled non-examinable in the source is retained with
that attribution; this is not a statement about the current year's assessment.
